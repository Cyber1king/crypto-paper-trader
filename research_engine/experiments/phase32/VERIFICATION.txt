  Baseline behaviour
    - Single-asset BTC/USDT 1h breakout-and-retest rule, 4-way entry label,
      opposite-signal exit only (max_holding, stop_loss, take_profit all None).
    - Signal computed on candles[:index]; filled at candles[index].open.
    - Exactly one open position at all times, enforced twice.
    - quantity = (cash * risk_fraction) / fill, risk_fraction of CURRENT cash.
    - cost_deduction: fee 0.001, slippage 0.0005 per leg, spread forced 0.0.
    - 6 paper modes, all implemented: standard, ai_intelligence, alerts,
      daily_target, manual, high_risk.
